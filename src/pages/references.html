Sources & credits

Every fact on this site is linked to a numbered source on its own page. This page collects all of those sources in one place, along with the data sets behind the charts and maps and a credit for every photograph.

How sources were chosen and checked

We relied first on U.S. government science agencies (NOAA, NASA, the National Park Service, EPA, USGS and the USDA Forest Service), the Chesapeake Bay Program, universities and peer-reviewed journals. Each source was opened and read to confirm that it supports the sentence it is attached to, and journal articles were checked against their DOI records. Where a source supports only part of a statement, the wording was narrowed to match it.

Numbers in the lists below follow the order in which each source first appears on that page, so they match the superscripts you see while reading.

Data behind the charts and maps

Climatograms
NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, U.S. Climate Normals 1991–2020, monthly and annual normals for Fairbanks International Airport, Alaska (station USW00026411) and Quillayute Airport, Washington (USW00094240). ncei.noaa.gov
River flow chart
U.S. Geological Survey, National Water Information System, monthly mean discharge for the Susquehanna River at Conowingo, Maryland (site 01578310). waterdata.usgs.gov
Forest maps
RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017 (Dinerstein et al. 2017), biomes “Boreal Forests/Taiga” and “Temperate Conifer Forests”, with Natural Earth coastlines, lakes and rivers. ecoregions.appspot.com
Estuary maps
Natural Earth public-domain coastlines (1:50 million scale for the world map, 1:10 million for the regional insets), lakes and rivers; estuary locations marked for this site. naturalearthdata.com
Diagrams
Original diagrams created for this site from the sources cited in each caption. They are schematic and not to scale.
