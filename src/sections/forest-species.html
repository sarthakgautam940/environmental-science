Characteristic plant and animal species

Six trees and seven animals, each explained the same way: the environmental challenge, the adaptation, how it works biologically, why it helps in this forest, and what it means for survival and reproduction.

An adaptation is an inherited trait that improves an organism’s chances of surviving and reproducing in its environment. In the boreal forest, the environment sets a short list of hard problems (cold, snow, a brief growing season, poor soil and fire) and the species below have solved them in different ways. Read each profile as a chain of cause and effect:

  1. Environmental challenge the problem the environment creates
  2. Adaptation the inherited trait
  3. How it works the biology behind it
  4. Why it helps here the link to this ecosystem
  5. Survival & reproduction the evolutionary payoff
