Characteristic plant and animal species

Four groups of producers and ten animals, each explained as a chain from environmental challenge to adaptation, mechanism, advantage and payoff.

Estuary organisms face problems that land organisms never do. The biggest is osmotic stress: water moves across cell membranes from where it is less salty to where it is more salty. When the surrounding water suddenly freshens on a rainy spring day, water floods into cells; when it turns salty again on the next tide, water is pulled out. NOAA divides organisms into two groups: stenohaline species tolerate only slight changes in salinity and usually live in either fresh or salt water, while euryhaline species tolerate a wide range. Euryhaline species are far fewer, because coping with constantly changing salinity takes a lot of energy.{{cite:noaa_est07}}
